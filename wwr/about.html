Rapids White Water Rafting

At Rapids White Water Rafting, our purpose is to create unforgettable river adventures. Our mission is to provide safe, exciting, and affordable rafting experiences for families and thrill-seekers alike. We live by our motto: Paddle Hard, Adventure Awaits! We believe the river brings people together and we are here to guide you every splash of the way.

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of river guides who loved adventure, Splash White Water Rafting started with just two rafts on the Colorado River. Since then, we have grown into one of the most trusted rafting companies in the region.

Our history is built on safety, passion, and respect for the great outdoors, leading thousands of guests through breathtaking canyons and exciting rapids year after year.
