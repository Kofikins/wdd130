Splash White Water Rafting

At Splash White Water Rafting, we are passionate about sharing the thrill of the river. Our goal is to give every guest a safe and memorable adventure on the water. Whether you are coming with your family for a fun trip or you are looking for an extreme white water experience, we have a tour for you. We believe rafting is more than just a sport, it is a way to make memories together. Come join us and let the river lead the way!

History

White Water Rapids began in 1998 with a small team of friends who shared a love for the river. We started with only two rafts on the Colorado River and a big dream. Over the years, we have worked hard to grow into a company that families and adventurers can trust for a safe and exciting experience.

For more than 25 years, we have focused on safety, adventure, and caring for nature. Every year, we have the privilege of guiding thousands of guests through beautiful canyons and powerful rapids, creating memories that last a lifetime.
